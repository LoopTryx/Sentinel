## Evidence semantics

[TinyUSB's HID implementation](https://github.com/hathach/tinyusb/blob/master/src/class/hid/hid_device.c) invokes the report-completion callback after a successful USB input transfer. That is transport evidence, not evidence a Windows application received a key. A filter that consumes then drops reports might protect applications while failing the owner's stricter no-host-consumption criterion. Keep both results separate; do not silently relax that criterion. Start-refusal versus report-dropping filter designs therefore require different acceptance reporting.

Required trial artifact set: firmware transfer timeline with a functioning independent channel and an unblocked positive control; unique first-marker sequence; device-specific Raw Input attribution; test-marker-only hook counters (a global low-level hook alone cannot identify a physical device); Notepad contents; bounded PnP/USB ETW with captured/lost-event accounting; live problem codes and setup state; internal/fallback control observations; exact policy/readback/recovery record; stable trial identifiers and evidence hashes. Missing, dead, overflowing or uncorrelated probes mean INCONCLUSIVE, never PASS. Raw user keystrokes, stable private device identifiers and unrelated trace contents must not be published or sent to AI.
