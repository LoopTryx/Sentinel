# School showcase and later delivery checkpoints

Owner update, 3 October 2026: the immediate goal is a reliable, impressive school-event demonstration. [Master plan section 20](Sentinel-Master-Plan.md#20-school-showcase--current-delivery-plan-3-october-2026) supersedes the previous broad preview checklist and defines current scope, acceptance and daily targets.

## S1 — School Showcase: immediate priority

Must-have: one launchable Y2K console; actual bounded benign selected-file/folder scans with reports/cancel/errors; local URL/scam evidence and synthetic examples; four polished Academy interactions (Password Autopsy, Phishing Dojo, URL X-Ray, USB Autopsy). GLM owns presentation, Codex logic/security, coordinator integration.

Should-have: stable passive Wi-Fi/hygiene or USB inventory. Stretch: eight lessons, synthetic mail/chat examples or secure password generation. Drop stretch before delaying the core. Static help is sufficient; a model/native host/live-site integration is optional until working and tested.

Use invented passwords/messages and benign sample files. Keep live/scenario/unavailable modes explicit. No daily-machine USB/HID blocking, destructive remediation, malware execution or CRYPTIC (E:) demonstration. Defender stays enabled. Real secrets or privileged operations still invoke their original safety gates.

Acceptance: pinned build/launcher and five-minute script; ten consecutive passing event-laptop rehearsals (at least three fresh launches and two offline); applicable cancel/partial/missing-component/privacy checks; core keyboard/readability checks; offline pack and clearly identified recording fallback. Section 20 gives exact acceptance and schedule. S1 can pass while protected R1/R2 remain incomplete.

## Phase 1-70 — later broader preview

Retain scanner/inspector, passive observations, native bridge, synthetic vault/message exercises, bounded monitoring, all 25 lessons and settings as the next preview roadmap. They are not school-event prerequisites. Use typed contracts and truthful states; fixtures do not become installed protection.

## Phase 1-30 — later capability/release closure

Protected USB requires recoverable hardware evidence. Quarantine requires identity/journal/recovery tests. Valuable vault/autofill use requires review. Installer/update distribution requires trust/lifecycle checks. Live-site claims require manual browser validation. These gates apply when delivering those capabilities, not to the smaller educational/read-only S1 milestone.

R0–R3 and all original tasks remain the long-term roadmap. 70/30 are checkpoint names, not engineering percentages. Preserve the original team schedule and attach revised Oct 2–24 S1 goals.
